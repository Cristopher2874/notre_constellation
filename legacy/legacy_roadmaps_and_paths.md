# Legacy Roadmaps & Alternative Paths Summary

*This document consolidates historical roadmaps and alternative professional paths explored prior to focusing strictly on the Assistive Robotics (Wandercraft) path.*

## 1. The 5-Month "Urban Microfarming Automation" Roadmap
*A past roadmap designed to build a portfolio-ready proof of concept for the Eiffel Scholarship application between April and August 2026.*

**Project Vision:** Design and build an automated urban farming system combining sprouting, vertical hydroponics, and composting automation for low-resource environments.
**Goal:** Demonstrate resource-constrained engineering, mechanical + software integration, and scalability.

**Key Phases:**
1. **Planning & Foundation (Apr):** System design, BOM (Bill of Materials), GitHub repository setup, and YouTube channel launch for documentation.
2. **Subsystem Design (May):** Prototyping individual modules (Sprouting, Composting, Hydroponic Garden) using Arduino/ESP32, moisture sensors, and pumps.
3. **Integration (Jun):** Connecting all subsystems to a master controller (ESP32) and building a web dashboard or app for monitoring/control.
4. **Optimization & Documentation (Jul):** Fine-tuning parameters, finalizing GitHub documentation, and writing a comprehensive technical white paper.
5. **Scholarship Prep (Aug):** Compiling the Becca Aiffel application package, focusing on French schools like INSA, Grenoble INP, École Polytechnique, and Centrale Lyon/Nantes.

## 2. Alternative Path: Autonomous Urban Resilience
*An alternative professional project focusing on energy and sustainability rather than assistive robotics.*

**Objective:** Design "Autonomous Resource Cells" — modular, self-sufficient environments managing their own energy, water, and domestic tasks via mechatronics and edge AI.
**Target Schools/Labs:** CentraleSupélec (L2S), Centrale Nantes (LS2N), Centrale Lyon (Ampère).

**Project Milestones:**
1. **Phase 1 (Energy Scavenging):** "Open-Source Microgrid Core". Building a 3D-printable wind turbine/solar tracker with custom PCB.
2. **Phase 2 (Predictive Management):** "Resource Intelligence Agent". Using Edge AI to predict energy peaks and automate appliances based on power availability.
3. **Phase 3 (Physical Autonomy):** "Self-Sufficient Cell". Using Computer Vision (OpenCV) to identify domestic tasks and coordinate actuators to save resources.

**Strategic Narrative:** Emphasized "Frugal Engineering" (solving low-resource problems with high-level AI logic) and "Sustainability as a System", demonstrating the ability to cross industrial practicalities in Mexico with advanced French research.

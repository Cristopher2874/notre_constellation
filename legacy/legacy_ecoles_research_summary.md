# Legacy Ecoles Research Summary

*This document consolidates historical research regarding the target Grandes Écoles (specifically the Écoles Centrales) for the Eiffel Excellence Scholarship application. It summarizes the findings from various initial research documents and infrastructure analyses.*

## The Eiffel Scholarship Context
- **Target:** France Excellence Eiffel Scholarship for a Master's/Double Degree.
- **Criteria:** Top 10% of class, GPA > 9.0 (UASLP), non-French nationality, B2/C1 French or C1 English.
- **Benefit:** 1,200 €/month, international travel, health insurance, priority housing.
- **UASLP Synergy:** Strong historical double-degree agreements with the Groupe des Écoles Centrales.

## Top 3 Target Écoles Centrales (for Mechatronics/Robotics)

### 1. École Centrale de Nantes (Centrale Nantes)
- **Focus:** Advanced Robotics, Marine/Offshore engineering, Additive Manufacturing.
- **Key Labs/Infrastructure:** LS2N (Laboratoire des Sciences du Numérique de Nantes). Houses over 450 researchers, autonomous vehicle platforms, drones, and medical cobots.
- **Relevant Programs:** MSc in Control and Robotics (CORO IMARO).
- **Strategic Fit:** The most direct focus on robotics within the group. Strong historical acceptance of UASLP Eiffel candidates.

### 2. CentraleSupélec (Université Paris-Saclay)
- **Focus:** Complex Systems, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Vision.
- **Key Labs/Infrastructure:** L2S (Signals and Systems), DataIA-Cluster. Deep integration with the Paris-Saclay "Deep Tech" ecosystem (Airbus, Renault, Thales).
- **Relevant Programs:** M2 Mechatronics, Machine Vision and Artificial Intelligence (MMVAI), Mobile Autonomous Systems.
- **Strategic Fit:** Unmatched industrial network and prestige (Top 2 engineering school in France). Ideal for merging AI with mechatronics.

### 3. École Centrale de Lyon (Centrale Lyon)
- **Focus:** Mechanical Engineering, Intelligent Automation, Embedded Systems, Power Electronics.
- **Key Labs/Infrastructure:** Ampère Lab (center of excellence for EEA - Electrical, Electronic, and Automation engineering). Unique capabilities in GaN/SiC components for power electronics.
- **Relevant Programs:** Master in Electronics, Electrical Energy and Automation (EEEA).
- **Strategic Fit:** Perfect balance between automation and electronics, providing flexibility for industrial mechatronics.

## Discarded Options
- **Centrale Lille:** Lower national ranking and focus heavily skewed toward logistics and transport rather than general-purpose robotics.
- **Centrale Méditerranée (Marseille):** Focuses heavily on the *Apprentissage* (work-study) model, which is often incompatible with the Eiffel Scholarship's full-time initial training requirement.
